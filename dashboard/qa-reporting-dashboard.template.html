Proof-of-concept reporting layer over the central QA pipeline — worst-of status rollup, Agency → Data Collection → Dataset → Column. Registry Services → Civil Registration → Birth Registrations is real: its figures are computed by running the actual dbt-core, Soda Core, datacontract-cli, and Evidently AI binaries against real generated pipeline runs. The Department for Child Protection and Family Support → Child Protection collection (all 6 datasets) is also real, computed the same way — including cross-table foreign-key and business-rule checks, not just single-column rules (see the "Real pipeline data" tag on each real dataset). Every other dataset on this page remains illustrative synthetic data for design review, not live production values.

Internal project codename: Mothman.

Cadence-aware viewing

View data asset as of a date

Rolls every real dataset back to whichever of its own QA runs was the most recent at or before the date you pick — showing exactly what was actually known as of that date, not just filtering what's on screen now. A dataset with no run yet as of that date shows as No data, never a misleading blank, green, or red. Applies to the whole data asset at once.
